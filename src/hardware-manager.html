🔎 Barcode Scanner

✓ No setup needed. A USB scanner acts as a keyboard - plug it in and it works.

Test your scanner

Click the box and scan any barcode. What the scanner typed appears below, exactly as the till receives it.

If a scan does not work

  • Nothing appears at all - the scanner is not in keyboard mode. Most scanners ship with a manual containing a "USB HID" or "keyboard wedge" barcode; scan that one.
  • Digits appear but the item is not found - the barcode is not on any item yet. Add it in the item's Barcode field.
  • The code appears but nothing happens until you press Enter - the scanner is not sending a return. Its manual has a "add Enter suffix" barcode.
  • Letters or symbols instead of digits - the scanner is set to a different keyboard layout than this computer. Set both to the same.

📺 Kitchen Screen

Run an HDMI cable from this computer to a screen in the kitchen. Choose it below, say how far away the cook stands, and this will tell you how many orders will actually be readable on it.

Looking for screens...

Why the distance matters more than the size

Text has to be a certain size on the eye, so doubling the distance doubles the letter height, and that costs room across and down. Double the distance, quarter the orders. Moving a screen closer is almost always cheaper than buying a bigger one, and a sharper screen does not help at all: a 4K panel the same size has the same letter height, just with smoother edges.
